🚋 HSL day ticket (trams, buses, Suomenlinna ferry)

  • One adult day ticket, zone AB, for each of you (~€10 each). The app sells one at a time, so buy it twice.
  • Start time must be 21.10.2026 09:00 (not 21:09). Check it in "Your tickets".
  • It runs 24 hours from the start, so it covers the whole day.
  • It's for Helsinki city transport only, not the ferries to Tallinn.
❓ Why zone AB?
HSL splits the region into rings. You can't buy zone A alone; AB is the smallest ticket.
ACentral Helsinki: every stop on this plan
BRest of Helsinki and nearest suburbs
CFurther out, including the airport: not needed
DFar edge of the region: not needed
🚋 How to use the tickets
  • Nothing to tap or scan. Get on any door of the tram or the Suomenlinna ferry.
  • The ticket switches on by itself at the start time. The yellow "Scheduled ticket" box turns into a live ticket.
  • If an inspector asks: HSL app → Tickets → Your tickets, show both. A screenshot won't do.
  • Fine without a valid ticket: about €100.
  • Both tickets are on Aman's phone, so stay together and keep it charged.
